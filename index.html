About Kipchimatt Supermarket & Wholesale

Kipchimatt Supermarket and Wholesale is a premier retail and bulk distribution giant headquartered in Kericho, Kenya. Established with a commitment to providing high quality everyday essentials at affordable rates, Kipchimatt has expanded aggressively across South Rift, Western, Nyanza, and Uasin Gishu counties.

Our operational model combines modern hypermarkets with heavy-duty wholesale warehouses, allowing us to serve both individual retail shoppers and bulk trade businesses under one brand umbrella.

Our Mission & Vision

Mission: To supply top-grade groceries, household products, and consumer goods at manufacturer-direct prices while generating employment and economic value in local Kenyan towns.

Vision: To become the leading retail and wholesale footprint in Western Kenya through continuous innovation, branch expansion, and community trust.

Our Core Principles

Affordability Unbeatable pricing across both retail shelves and wholesale bulk packs.
Opportunity Creating fair, inclusive jobs for Kenyans at every qualification level.
Integrity Transparent customer care, genuine stock sourcing, and ethical employment.
